# 06 · Red-team review of study design v2, and responses

*An independent adversarial review of `04_study_design_v2.md` (v2), done after the design was written and without access to its authors' reasoning. Part A is the review as received; finding IDs 1–18 are cited as R1–R18 in `04` v2.1. Part B is the response to each finding.*

## Part A · Review (as received, of v2)

Reviewer stance: tough journal referee and senior data-science lead. The repository was not edited. One check was run on a scratch copy of `pilot/`: an M2 refit on the GD fork, which confirmed finding 1, and a build, which exposed finding 10.

## Verdict (3 lines)
The design is far stronger than the proposal, but its central estimand is double-centred by construction. That silently undercuts the centrality, "rivalry" and "bloc contamination solved" claims, and every jury-vs-televote comparison is biased by differential shrinkage.
The "pre-registration" is not one: the hypotheses were written after the pilot saw the answers, and the timeline runs the main RQ1–RQ3 estimates *before* the freeze.
Scope is unrealistic for two students by Jan 27, with every core model still `NotImplementedError`. Cut the MVP hard, fix the estimand wording, and move covariates into the likelihood.
